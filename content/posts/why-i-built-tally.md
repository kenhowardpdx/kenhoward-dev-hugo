Every banking app tells you the same thing: here's your balance, right now, today.

What it doesn't tell you is what that balance looks like in nine days, after rent clears,
the car payment goes out, and that annual subscription you forgot about renews. By the
time you find out you're short, you're already short.

I've spent the last several months building a tool that flips this around. Instead of
looking backward at where your money went, it looks forward — projecting your balance
day by day based on your recurring bills and actual spending patterns, so you can see a
dip coming before it happens instead of after.

It's called [Tally, a cash flow forecasting tool](https://tallyahead.com). It connects to your
accounts, maps out your recurring bills, and forecasts your balance days or weeks ahead, so a
shortfall shows up as a warning instead of a surprise. It's in early access now, and if you've
ever been caught off guard by your own bank account, I'd love for you to try it.
